Health Checkup Packages in Koppa — Which One Do You Need?

Adarsh Hospital in Koppa, Chikkamagaluru, offers tailored preventive health checkup packages (Mini, Master, and Executive Master) comprising vital blood chemistries, organ function screens, and advanced cardiac/ultrasound diagnostics, designed to detect health concerns early.

Operating in Koppa since 2003, Adarsh Hospital has treated over 15,000 patients under its NABH-accredited since 2018 standards, providing 9 structured health checkup packages. Modern lifestyle shifts and rising environmental factors have made preventive healthcare a necessity rather than an option. Early detection is the single most effective shield against chronic illnesses like diabetes, cardiac disease, and hypertension. In the Koppa and greater Chikkamagaluru region, Adarsh Hospital has established specialized, comprehensive screening suites designed to identify potential physiological warning signs before they become clinical emergencies.

Why Choose a Preventive Health Package?

Many critical health conditions—such as high blood pressure, elevated blood sugar, liver enzyme imbalances, or renal filtration issues—typically develop silently, showing no noticeable symptoms in their early stages. A routine laboratory and radiological screening maps key blood metrics, organ functions, and cardiac indicators, allowing doctors to detect subtle abnormalities and design preventive treatment pathways.

Comparing the Packages: Which is Right for You?

At Adarsh Hospital, we offer a range of checkup packages designed to suit different age groups, risk factors, and budgets. Understanding the focus of each package helps you make an informed decision:

  • Mini Health Checkup (₹1,500): Ideal for young adults (under 30) seeking a basic health baseline. It covers essential parameters including complete blood count, blood sugar, lipid profiles, and urine analysis, alongside a physician consultation.
  • Master Health Checkup (₹3,000): A balanced full-body screening suitable for individuals aged 30 to 45. In addition to basic laboratory tests, it includes critical diagnostic evaluations such as ECG, liver function tests, and renal profiles.
  • Executive Master Health Checkup (₹5,000): Our most advanced, premium full-body mapping. Highly recommended for individuals over 45 or those with family histories of cardiovascular or metabolic diseases. It features 19 key tests including 2D Echo, Treadmill stress test (TMT), ultrasound scanning, thyroid audits, and specialized consultations.

For more detailed parameter breakdowns and additional health screenings (like diabetic, kidney stone, or women's wellness packages), please visit our dedicated Health Checkup Packages page.

Preparing for Your Health Checkup

To ensure maximum accuracy of your clinical readings, follow these guidelines prior to visiting the hospital:

  • Ensure a strict overnight fasting period of 10 to 12 hours. Only plain water is permitted during this time.
  • Avoid consuming alcohol or high-fat meals for at least 24 hours prior to the exam.
  • Bring any current prescription medications or previous health records to discuss with our physician.
