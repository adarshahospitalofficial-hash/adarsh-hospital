General & Laparoscopic Surgery in Koppa

Adarsh Hospital's General and Laparoscopic Surgery Department in Koppa, Chikkamagaluru, offers comprehensive minor, major, and minimally invasive (keyhole) procedures. The surgical team operates in sterile, modern theatres with full anesthesiology support for rapid, safe recovery.

Clinical Source: Led by senior surgeons Dr. Shiva Kumar, MBBS MS and Dr. Rajesh Bhakta, MBBS MD, with expert anesthesia care from Dr. Anitha N. Rao, MBBS MD DA DNB (KMC Reg. No. 35287).

Frequently Asked Questions

What laparoscopic surgeries are performed at Adarsh Hospital?

Our surgical team performs keyhole procedures including laparoscopic appendectomy, cholecystectomy for gallstones, and hernia repairs, minimizing recovery times.

Are emergency surgeries available 24/7?

Yes, Adarsh Hospital provides round-the-clock emergency surgical support for acute cases such as appendicitis, trauma, and bowel obstructions.

Who are the surgeons at Adarsh Hospital?

Our surgical theatre procedures are led by highly qualified and experienced specialists:

  • Dr. Shiva Kumar, MBBS MS: Senior general surgeon, specializing in complex open and emergency surgeries.
  • Dr. Rajesh Bhakta, MBBS MD: Laparoscopic surgery specialist, focusing on minimally invasive keyhole procedures.
  • Dr. Anitha N. Rao, MBBS MD DA DNB (KMC Reg. No. 35287): Senior consultant anesthesiologist, managing patient sedation, critical care, and post-operative pain relief.
