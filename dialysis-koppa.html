Dialysis Centre - Adarsh Hospital Koppa

Adarsh Hospital's Dialysis Centre in Koppa, Chikkamagaluru, offers renal replacement therapy with a Zero-reuse dialyzer policy, RO-filtered water, and nephrologist supervision, in collaboration with Lions Seva Trust. The centre is open for scheduled and emergency dialysis sessions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is dialysis available in Koppa?

Yes, advanced renal replacement therapy is available at Adarsh Hospital's Dialysis Centre in Koppa, Chikkamagaluru, operated in collaboration with the Lions Seva Trust.

Does Adarsh Hospital reuse dialyzers?

No, Adarsh Hospital follows a strict Zero-reuse dialyzer policy, utilizing brand new single-use dialyzers for every single dialysis session to ensure patient safety, prevent infection, and maximize treatment efficiency. This renal service is operated in collaboration with Lions Seva Trust.

What is the frequency of dialysis sessions?

Most patients require dialysis sessions three times a week, with each session lasting approximately 4 hours, customized to the nephrologist's clinical prescription.
