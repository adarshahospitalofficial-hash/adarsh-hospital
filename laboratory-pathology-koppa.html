Pathology Laboratory in Koppa

Frequently Asked Questions

What diagnostic tests can I get at Adarsha Hospital?

Our laboratory offers complete diagnostics, including CBC, diabetes profiles (HbA1c), liver/kidney function tests, thyroid panels, lipid profiles, and urine analysis.

Are lab testing services available on Sundays?

Yes, the laboratory at Adarsha Hospital in Koppa is open on Sundays from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM for routine tests, with 24/7 support for emergency diagnostics.

How long does it take to get laboratory test results?

Adarsha Hospital is committed to providing rapid diagnostic reports. The typical turnaround times are as follows:

  • Routine Blood & Urine Tests (e.g., CBC, FBS, Creatinine): Generated within 2 to 3 hours of sample collection.
  • Specialized Assays & Hormone Panels (e.g., Thyroid, HbA1c): Typically ready within 4 hours.
  • Emergency Critical Lab Alerts: Completed immediately within 30 to 45 minutes for emergency ward patients.
