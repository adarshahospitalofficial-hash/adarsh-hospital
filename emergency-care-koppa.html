Frequently Asked Questions

Is emergency and trauma care available 24/7 in Koppa?

Yes, Adarsh Hospital provides 24/7 continuous emergency room support, trauma care, and laboratory/pharmacy services in Koppa.

How do I request emergency ambulance dispatch?

For immediate emergency ambulance assistance in Koppa and surrounding areas, please follow these steps:

  1. Call our dedicated 24x7 ambulance hotline immediately at +91 94485 86099.
  2. Provide your exact location and landmarks in or around Koppa.
  3. Briefly describe the patient's condition (e.g., trauma, chest pain, labor) so the paramedics can prepare the necessary life-support equipment.
  4. Keep your phone line free so our ambulance driver or coordinator can call you back for navigation updates.
Which doctors supervise the emergency care department?

The emergency department is directed by general surgeon Dr. Shiva Kumar, MBBS MS, and physician Dr. Ismail, MBBS MD.
