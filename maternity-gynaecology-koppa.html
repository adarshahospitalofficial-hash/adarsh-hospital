Maternity & Gynaecology Care in Koppa

Adarsha Hospital's Maternity and Gynaecology department in Koppa, Chikkamagaluru, provides comprehensive women's health services including prenatal care, maternal supervision, pelvic ultrasound scans, infertility workups, and neonatal stabilization. The department supports both normal and high-risk deliveries.

Clinical Source: Led by senior obstetrician Dr. Nataraj R. Rao, MBBS MD DNB (KMC Reg. No. 31199) and anesthesiologist Dr. Anitha N. Rao, MBBS MD DA DNB (KMC Reg. No. 35287).

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the gynaecologist at Adarsha Hospital?

Women's healthcare at Adarsha Hospital is led by senior consultant obstetrician and gynaecologist Dr. Nataraj R. Rao, MBBS MD DNB, KMC Reg. No. 31199.

Is a NICU available for newborns in Koppa?

Yes, Adarsha Hospital is equipped with a dedicated Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) with warmers and phototherapy to care for premature and vulnerable infants in Koppa.

What maternity services are provided?

Adarsha Hospital's Maternity & Gynaecology department offers comprehensive care pathways, including:

  • Antenatal Care (Pregnancy Monitoring): Regular consultations, weight/BP checks, and fetal health audits.
  • Delivery Services: Support for painless labor, normal deliveries, and emergency caesarean sections (C-sections).
  • Newborn Support: Immediate postnatal screening and access to our Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU).
  • Gynaecological Audits: Diagnostic ultrasound scans, PCOD/PCOS therapeutic plans, infertility workups, and women's cancer screening.
