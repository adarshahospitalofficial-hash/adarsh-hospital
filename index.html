About Hospital

Adarsh Hospital – Koppa's Trusted Multispecialty Hospital

Adarsh Hospital is a leading NABH-accredited multispecialty healthcare centre in Koppa, serving 15,000+ patients since 2003. As one of the top-rated hospitals in Chikkamagaluru district, we deliver advanced diagnostics, laparoscopic surgery, emergency trauma response, critical care, and dialysis services to the malnad community.

Content last updated: July 27, 2026

Operating in Koppa since 2003, Adarsh Hospital is a trusted, NABH-accredited since 2018 multispecialty healthcare centre that has treated over 15,000 patients across 10+ specialists and 9 structured health checkup packages. Our team of qualified doctors, radiologists, and technologists work together with modern equipment to provide dependable clinical outcomes for the Chikkamagaluru district and the wider malnad community.

From advanced radiology and cardiology to 24×7 laboratory services (providing "24×7 laboratory medicine" turnaround) and specialist consultations, patients now have access to comprehensive care without long journeys to larger cities.

Financial Ease

Cashless Facilities & Schemes

Avail cashless hospitalization and surgical treatments through leading government health schemes and private insurance providers.

Government Scheme

Yeshasvini Health Scheme

Adarsh Hospital is an impanelled provider for the prestigious Yeshasvini Health Card Scheme, facilitating cashless surgical and medical treatments for co-operative society members in Karnataka.

  • Cashless Surgery: Covers a wide list of general surgeries, laparoscopic procedures, and specialty care.
  • Eligible Members: Open to members of rural and urban co-operative societies, self-help groups, and weavers' associations.
  • Outpatient Benefits: Structured diagnostic and consultation subsidies as per scheme guidelines.

How to Avail: Present your valid Yeshasvini card along with your Co-operative Society passbook and identity proof (Aadhaar Card) at our reception counter during admission.

Third-Party Cashless

Private Insurance & TPAs

We offer hassle-free cashless hospitalization facilities in collaboration with leading Third-Party Administrators (TPAs) and corporate health insurance companies.

  • Pre-Authorization Support: Dedicated desk to submit pre-auth documents and secure approvals within 2-4 hours.
  • Emergency Admissions: Quick processing for emergency trauma, cardiology, and maternity cases.
  • Minimal Out-of-Pocket: Direct billing to insurers, leaving only non-medical expenses for payment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about appointments, laboratory reporting, and clinical services.

What are the hospital working hours?
Our general outpatient consultations and diagnostic services run:
Monday - Saturday: 9:00 AM – 2:00 PM and 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM
Sunday: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM.
Note that emergency care and critical ambulance dispatch operate 24x7.
How can I book a health checkup package?
You can book any of our 9 health checkup packages by calling our hotlines directly (+91 63609 93646 / +91 70199 48944 / +91 94485 86099) or by filling out the "Request a Callback" form in the Contact section of our website.
Are emergency services available 24 hours a day?
Yes, Adarsh Hospital provides round-the-clock (24/7) emergency care, trauma management, critical care support, and medical ambulance services.
Where is Shalini Diagnostics located?
Shalini Diagnostics & Health Centre is a division of Adarsh Hospital and is located near Guru Bhavana, Main Road, Koppa – 577126. Its laboratory services open early from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM daily.
Is Adarsh Hospital accredited?
Yes, Adarsh Hospital is a proud NABH-accredited since 2018 hospital, upholding supreme standards of patient safety, clinical quality, hygiene, and ethical medical care.
Is Adarsh Hospital one of the leading hospitals in Koppa?
Yes, Adarsh Hospital is one of the leading NABH-accredited multispecialty hospitals in Koppa, Chikkamagaluru, delivering 24/7 emergency response, advanced diagnostics, and specialist surgery.
Why is Adarsh Hospital considered a preferred medical facility in Koppa?
Adarsh Hospital is a preferred medical facility in Koppa due to its comprehensive outpatient consultations, advanced ICU facilities, state-of-the-art dialysis, and round-the-clock emergency trauma response, backed by over two decades of trusted service.
Does Adarsh Hospital support the Yeshasvini Health Scheme?
