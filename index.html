Defined by Our Excellence

Welcome to Adarsha Hospital, Koppa

Located in Koppa, Chikkamagaluru district of Karnataka, Adarsha Hospital is a trusted multispecialty healthcare centre committed to delivering accurate diagnosis, quality treatment and warm patient care. Our team of qualified doctors, radiologists and technologists work together with modern equipment to provide dependable clinical outcomes for the malnad community.

From advanced radiology and cardiology to 24×7 laboratory services and specialist consultations, patients now have access to comprehensive care without long journeys to larger cities.
