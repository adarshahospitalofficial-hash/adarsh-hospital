About Hospital

Welcome to ADARSH HOSPITAL, Koppa

Located in Koppa, Chikkamagaluru district of Karnataka, ADARSH HOSPITAL is a trusted, NABH-accredited multispecialty healthcare centre committed to delivering accurate diagnosis, quality treatment and warm patient care. Our team of qualified doctors, radiologists and technologists work together with modern equipment to provide dependable clinical outcomes for the malnad community.

From advanced radiology and cardiology to 24×7 laboratory services and specialist consultations, patients now have access to comprehensive care without long journeys to larger cities.

Complete Range of Services

Detailed list of outpatient, emergency, diagnostic, and specialty medical services available at ADARSH HOSPITAL.

1. Outpatient Consultation

Expert medical opinions and follow-ups across primary, medicine, surgery, and maternal health specialties.

2. Emergency Services

24x7 trauma care, accident response, acute cardiac stabilization, and urgent surgical treatments.

3. Inpatient Department (IPD)

Comfortable recovery wards with round-the-clock nursing supervision and bedside medical monitoring.

4. Intensive Care Unit (ICU)

Critical care support equipped with ventilators, defibrillators, multi-para monitors, and specialized staff.

5. Neonatal ICU (NICU)

Dedicated clinical environment with phototherapy and warming pods for premature and high-risk newborns.

6. Maternity Ward & OT

Labor suites and modern operation theatres configured for safe Caesarean and standard deliveries.

7. Clinical Laboratory

NABH-compliant automated biochemistry, hematology, immunology, and hormone assays.

8. In-House Pharmacy

Stocked with genuine drugs, critical medicines, surgical supplies, and patient healthcare products.

9. Radiology & Imaging

Diagnostics including multi-slice CT scanning, digital X-rays, and multi-dimensional ultrasound studies.

10. Cardiology Services

Advanced non-invasive cardiac checkups including ECG, Treadmill Stress Testing (TMT), and 2D Echocardiograms.

11. Ultrasound Scan (USG)

Highly detailed anomaly scans, follicular monitoring, pelvic, and abdominal ultrasonography.

12. Ambulance Service

Round-the-clock clinical transit ambulance equipped with oxygen cylinders and emergency medical kits.

13. Maternal & Women's Health

Specialized treatments for menstrual problems, infertility, PCOD, hormonal imbalances, and menopause.

14. Laparoscopy Surgery

Minimally invasive keyhole surgeries for appendix, gall bladder, hernia, and reproductive organs.

15. Visiting Senior Consultants

Convenient local consultations from visiting senior specialists in orthopedics, urology, and neurology.

16. Ophthalmology Clinic

Detailed eye examinations, vision testing, glaucoma screening, and management of diabetic retinopathy.

17. Pulmonary Care

Asthma management, chronic bronchitis care, and diagnostic Pulmonary Function Tests (PFT).

18. Kidney & Urinary Care

Diagnosis of kidney stones, urinary tract infections (UTI), and metabolic renal disorders.

19. Preventive Health Checkups

Structured medical packages designed for early detection of systemic disorders and cardiac checkups.

20. Vaccination Services

Essential childhood immunizations, pediatric vaccines, and adult protective vaccinations.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about appointments, laboratory reporting, and clinical services.

What are the hospital working hours?
Our general outpatient consultations and diagnostic services run:
Monday - Saturday: 9:00 AM – 2:00 PM and 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM
Sunday: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM.
Note that emergency care and critical ambulance dispatch operate 24x7.
How can I book a health checkup package?
You can book any of our 9 health checkup packages by calling our hotlines directly (+91 63609 93646 / +91 70199 48944) or by filling out the "Request a Callback" form in the Contact section of our website.
Are emergency services available 24 hours a day?
Yes, ADARSH HOSPITAL provides round-the-clock (24/7) emergency care, trauma management, critical care support, and medical ambulance services.
Where is Shalini Diagnostics located?
Shalini Diagnostics & Health Centre is a division of ADARSH HOSPITAL and operates within the same medical campus at Ambedkar Road, Upperpet, Koppa. Its laboratory services open early from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM daily.
Is ADARSH HOSPITAL accredited?
Yes, ADARSH HOSPITAL is a proud NABH Accredited Hospital, upholding supreme standards of patient safety, clinical quality, hygiene, and ethical medical care.
