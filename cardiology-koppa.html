Frequently Asked Questions

What cardiac tests are available at Adarsh Hospital?

Adarsh Hospital offers non-invasive cardiac diagnostics to evaluate heart function and detect cardiovascular diseases. Key tests include:

  • Electrocardiogram (ECG): A quick test that records the electrical signals of your heart to detect arrhythmias and ischemia.
  • 2D Echocardiogram (Echo): An ultrasound scan of the heart that provides detailed images of heart valves, chambers, and pumping capacity.
  • Treadmill Test (TMT): A cardiac stress test conducted while walking on a treadmill to evaluate how your heart responds to physical exertion.
Is TMT testing available in Koppa?

Yes, Treadmill Test (TMT) or cardiac stress testing is available locally at Adarsh Hospital in Koppa, Chikkamagaluru, eliminating the need to travel to distant cities.

How do I prepare for a Treadmill Test (TMT)?

To prepare for a Treadmill Test (TMT) at Adarsh Hospital, please follow these step-by-step instructions:

  1. Wear comfortable loose clothing and walking shoes.
  2. Avoid heavy meals, tea, coffee, or caffeine for at least 3 hours before the test.
  3. Consult our cardiologists/doctors regarding your current heart medications.
  4. Bring your past medical reports and ECGs if available.
