Frequently Asked Questions

Does Adarsh Hospital have a NICU?

Yes, Adarsh Hospital features a dedicated Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) equipped with incubators, warming pods, and phototherapy to care for premature or ill infants in Koppa.

Do you offer child immunization services?

Yes, Adarsh Hospital provides comprehensive pediatric vaccination programs in accordance with national immunization guidelines.

What is the difference between a NICU and a standard nursery?

Understanding the key differences between the two newborn environments helps parents prepare for delivery:

  • Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU): A specialized critical care ward with high-level continuous monitoring, advanced thermoregulation, phototherapy, and respiratory assistance for premature (born before 37 weeks) or high-risk babies.
  • Regular Newborn Nursery: A standard clinical setting for healthy, full-term babies (37 to 42 weeks of gestation) who need routine monitoring, pediatric checkups, and standard post-birth stabilization before discharge.
